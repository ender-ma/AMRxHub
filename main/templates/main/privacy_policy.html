This Privacy and Data Protection Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect personal data when you use our website and services.

This Policy is intended to reflect the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and applicable regulatory guidance. It should be read together with our Terms of Use and Cookie Policy.

1. Who We Are

AMRx Hub is responsible for processing personal data collected through the platform where we determine the purposes and means of processing. For privacy questions, requests, or complaints, contact us at amrtool344@gmail.com.

2. Scope of This Policy

This Policy applies to personal data processed through the AMRx Hub website, web application, account system, support communications, and related services. It does not apply to third-party websites, databases, software, publications, or other resources linked from our platform. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

3. Personal Data We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide

  • Name and email address provided during registration.
  • Information submitted when you contact support, provide feedback, or communicate with us.
  • Information voluntarily provided through features that are available and clearly described to you.

3.2 Technical and Usage Information

  • IP address, security logs, and technical information collected by our application or infrastructure.
  • Browser, device, operating-system, and similar technical information.
  • Pages or resources accessed, timestamps, referring information, and usage information generated through normal operation.
  • Cookie and similar-technology information, as described in our Cookie Policy.

AMRx Hub does not require patient medical records or health histories for ordinary directory use. Please do not submit patient-identifying health information through features that are not expressly designed and enabled for that purpose.

4. How We Use Personal Data

We may use personal data to create and administer accounts; provide, maintain, and improve our services; respond to support requests; send service, security, account, or policy notices; prevent abuse and protect platform security; understand usage and performance; comply with legal obligations; and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

5. Lawful Bases for Processing

Depending on the activity and applicable law, we may process personal data based on consent, performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, or legitimate interests that are balanced against your rights and interests. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent, subject to any lawful reason we may have to continue processing.

6. Data Accuracy and Minimisation

We seek to collect personal data that is adequate, relevant, and limited to what is necessary for identified purposes. Please provide accurate information and notify us when your account information needs correction.

7. Sharing and Disclosure

We may share personal data with service providers that support hosting, email delivery, security, analytics, infrastructure, or other platform operations. We may also disclose information to authorities, courts, or regulators where required or permitted by law, or to parties involved in a lawful corporate transaction. We do not sell personal data.

8. International Transfers

Some service providers or infrastructure used by AMRx Hub may process personal data outside Nigeria. Where personal data is transferred across borders, we will apply the safeguards and lawful transfer mechanisms required by applicable law.

9. Cookies

We may use essential cookies and similar technologies for authentication, security, session management, and core functionality. Where non-essential analytics or similar technologies are used, we will provide the notices and choices required by applicable law. More information is available in our Cookie Policy.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risks of processing personal data, including access controls, secure authentication, encryption in transit, logging, backups, and other safeguards. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

If a personal-data breach occurs, we will assess and respond to it in accordance with applicable law and our incident-response procedures, including making any required notifications.

11. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, to meet legal or regulatory obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, maintain security, or otherwise as permitted by law.

12. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law and verification requirements, you may have the right to access, correct, erase, or restrict the processing of your personal data; object to certain processing; request data portability; withdraw consent; and exercise other rights available under Nigerian data-protection law.

To exercise your rights, contact amrtool344@gmail.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

13. Sensitive Personal Data

AMRx Hub does not require patient health records for its ordinary resource-discovery function. Users must not submit identifiable patient records or other sensitive personal data unless we expressly enable the relevant feature and provide an appropriate framework for it.

Our platform provides scientific and educational resources and is not a healthcare provider. Information available through AMRx Hub is not a substitute for professional medical or clinical advice.

14. Third-Party Resources

AMRx Hub links to third-party resources that we do not control. When you leave our platform or interact with an external resource, that third party may collect information under its own privacy policy and terms.

15. Children’s Privacy

AMRx Hub is intended for users who can lawfully use the platform and enter into any required contractual relationship. If we knowingly collect personal data from a child in circumstances requiring additional protections, we will apply the safeguards required by applicable law.

16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when our services, processing activities, or applicable law changes. The latest version will display its effective date. Where a change is material and notice or consent is required, we will provide the appropriate notice or obtain the required consent.

17. Contact and Complaints

For privacy or data-protection questions, requests, or complaints, contact amrtool344@gmail.com. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or another competent authority under applicable law.
