Overview

AMRx Hub (the “Platform”) is committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with Nigeria’s Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the National Health Act 2014 (NHA). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how we safeguard and use it. AMRx Hub is based in Nigeria and operates as a curated directory of antimicrobial resistance (AMR) resources. We do not host or operate AMR analysis tools; we link to third‑party tools, PDFs, and videos. By using the Platform you consent to the practices described below. The policy is written to be clear, concise, transparent and easily accessible, in line with NDPA requirements.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you register we collect your name, email address and password. We verify your email to create a secure account. This data is used to manage your account, authenticate you, and communicate important notices. You may update or delete this information via account settings.
  • Usage data and cookies: We automatically collect technical and usage data (for example: browser type, device type, pages visited, and timestamps) to operate and improve the Platform. We also use cookies for essential functions (session management) and for performance analytics (e.g., Google Analytics). Cookies may be session or persistent.
  • Other information: We do not currently collect sensitive personal data (such as health or biometric data). If we later permit user-generated content (forums, uploads) and you provide health-related information, it will be treated as sensitive and protected with additional safeguards and explicit consent.

Data minimization: we collect only what is necessary. We do not request health history or identifiers beyond what is required for account registration and service delivery. All processing complies with the NDPA’s requirement that processing be for a specific, legitimate and lawful purpose disclosed to the user.

How We Use Your Information

We use personal data for the following purposes:

  • Account management: verify email addresses, secure accounts, and provide Platform features.
  • Communications: send important notices (password resets, policy updates) and reply to support inquiries.
  • Platform improvement: analyze aggregated usage data to troubleshoot, optimize and enhance user experience. Analytics are aggregated and anonymized where possible.
  • Legal compliance: respond to lawful requests and meet regulatory obligations.

We will not process your personal information for purposes incompatible with the NDPA without further consent. Lawful processing bases include: your consent, performance of a contract (Terms of Service), legal obligations, and our legitimate interests (platform administration and improvement), applied proportionately and respecting your rights.

Legal Bases for Processing

  1. Consent: you have given clear consent for specific processing activities (e.g., account setup, newsletters). You may withdraw consent anytime.
  2. Contract: processing necessary to perform our Terms of Service (maintain accounts, provide directory services).
  3. Legal obligation: processing required to comply with law (for example, record retention).
  4. Legitimate interest: for platform administration and improvement, when balanced against your rights.

We do not rely on sensitive data bases (e.g., health) without explicit additional consent. We do not sell or trade personal data.

Information Sharing and Recipients

We do not sell personal information. We share data only in limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: trusted third‑party vendors (email delivery, hosting, analytics) that perform services on our behalf. They are contractually bound to protect data and use it only to provide agreed services.
  • Legal and safety disclosures: where required by law (court orders, lawful requests) or to protect rights, property or safety.
  • Business transfers: in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale, personal data may transfer to the new owner under similar privacy obligations.

When transferring personal data outside Nigeria we ensure compliance with NDPA cross‑border rules: transfers are made only to jurisdictions with adequate protection or using safeguards (e.g., contractual clauses) and with documented legal bases.

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance site functionality and measure performance. Summary:

  • Essential cookies: required for login and core functionality (session management).
  • Performance/analytics cookies: anonymized data collection (e.g., Google Analytics) to understand usage and improve the Platform.
  • Functional cookies: store preferences (for example, language).
  • No marketing cookies (currently): AMRx Hub does not run marketing or advertising trackers at this time. If introduced, they will be disclosed and consent requested where required.

You may control cookies through browser settings or via our consent banner where provided. Blocking cookies may limit certain features.

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or destruction. Examples include secure servers, encryption for data in transit, and access controls. We regularly review security practices to address emerging threats. Although we apply safeguards, no system is completely immune to breaches. If a breach is likely to pose a high risk to rights and freedoms, we will follow NDPA breach notification procedures and inform the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) and affected users as required.

Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described or as required by law. When an account is deleted or inactive for an extended period, personal information will be erased or anonymized. Retention periods may vary by purpose (for example, contact logs may be kept for 2–3 years for support). NDPA requires that data not be kept longer than needed for its original purpose; where no legitimate reason remains we will remove the data.

Your Rights

Under the NDPA you have rights over your personal data. These include:

  • Access & rectification: request confirmation and a copy of data we hold; ask for correction of inaccuracies.
  • Erasure (right to be forgotten): request deletion when data is no longer necessary or consent withdrawn and no other legal basis applies.
  • Restriction: request suspension or limitation of processing (e.g., while accuracy is contested).
  • Data portability: request a structured, commonly used and machine-readable copy of your data.
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent: withdraw consent for specific processing at any time; we will stop processing based on that consent.
  • Complain to authorities: lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) or other relevant authority.

To exercise any of these rights contact us using the details below. We will respond within NDPA timelines and without unreasonable delay.

Sensitive & Health-Related Information

Although AMRx Hub does not collect patient health records, we recognise the sensitivity of health information. Under the National Health Act, health information is confidential. If you provide health-related information (for example, via feedback or future uploads), we will treat it as sensitive personal data and process it only with explicit consent and additional safeguards as required by law. AMRx Hub is not a healthcare provider and does not provide medical advice; content is for informational and educational purposes only.
