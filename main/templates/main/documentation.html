Introduction

What is AMRx Hub?

AMRx Hub is a central platform designed to provide tools, resources, and guidance for antimicrobial resistance (AMR) research and collaboration.

Who should use this documentation?

Researchers, healthcare professionals, and policymakers working on AMR topics who want structured guidance on using the Hub.
