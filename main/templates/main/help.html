Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats are supported for upload?

CSV, Excel (.xlsx, .xls), and XML are supported. You can also download templates from the Resources section to ensure correct formatting.

How do I interpret resistance results?

Results follow CLSI and EUCAST guidelines. They are classified as Susceptible (S), Intermediate (I), or Resistant (R). More details can be found in our Resources.

Can I export my reports?

Yes, you can export reports as PDF, Excel, or send them directly to your email. Exports include graphs, charts, and summaries.

How secure is my data?

Your data is protected with enterprise-grade encryption and stored securely. We comply with HIPAA and GDPR standards.

Tips & Best Practices

Organize Your Data Effectively

Ensure your datasets are clean, well-labeled, and consistently formatted. Use standard headers and separate categorical and numerical data properly. A well-structured dataset reduces analysis errors and speeds up processing.

Validate Before Upload

Use AMRx Hub's provided templates to check your data for missing values, incorrect formats, or inconsistent units. Validation before upload ensures smooth analysis and reliable results.

Interpret Results Carefully

Cross-reference the analysis outputs with CLSI and EUCAST guidelines. Pay attention to borderline resistance cases, and consider the clinical context when reviewing the results.

Avoid Common Mistakes

Do not mix data types in a single column, avoid duplicate entries, and ensure sample IDs are unique. Following these best practices prevents errors and improves the reliability of your analysis.

Leverage Curated Tools

Use AMRx Hub's curated tools for data visualization, pattern recognition, and reporting. Familiarize yourself with each tool’s purpose to make your workflow efficient and insightful.
