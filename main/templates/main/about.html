Our Mission

To become the world's most comprehensive and trusted platform for antimicrobial resistance knowledge, tools, and data, empowering researchers, clinicians, educators, and innovators to advance solutions against antimicrobial resistance.

Our Vision

To reduce fragmentation in the antimicrobial resistance ecosystem by providing a curated, searchable, and continuously updated hub that connects researchers and stakeholders with the resources needed to accelerate antimicrobial resistance discovery, surveillance, education, and innovation.

Who Is This For?

AMRx Hub is designed for anyone working, learning, or interested in antimicrobial resistance who needs a reliable way to discover, explore, and access AMR-related tools and resources.

Researchers and Academics

Discover curated AMR databases, analytical tools, publications, datasets, and other resources to support research, teaching, and scientific investigation.

Bioinformaticians and Data Scientists

Find computational tools, databases, pipelines, and data resources for analysing microbial genomes, resistance determinants, surveillance data, and other AMR-related datasets.

Clinical Microbiologists and Laboratory Professionals

Access resources that support laboratory research, antimicrobial resistance analysis, microbial identification, resistance surveillance, and interpretation of microbiological data.

Epidemiologists and Public Health Professionals

Explore AMR surveillance resources, epidemiological tools, datasets, and analytical platforms that can support the monitoring and understanding of resistance patterns across populations and settings.

Students and Educators

Use the Hub to discover educational materials, tutorials, publications, databases, and practical tools for learning and teaching antimicrobial resistance and related microbiological concepts.

Healthcare and One Health Professionals

Explore resources relevant to antimicrobial resistance across human health, animal health, food systems, and the environment, supporting a broader One Health understanding of AMR.

One Hub, Multiple Uses

Whether you are looking for a tool to analyse data, a database to explore, a publication to read, or educational material to learn from, AMRx Hub brings AMR-related resources together in one searchable platform.

Key Features

What makes AMRx Hub a useful starting point for AMR work.

Web-Based Access

Access AMRx Hub directly through a modern web browser without requiring software downloads or local installation. The Hub provides a centralized online environment for discovering antimicrobial resistance tools and resources.

Curated AMR Resources

Explore a growing collection of carefully organized AMR-related tools, databases, publications, educational materials, and other resources from across the field.

Structured Resource Categories

Resources are organized into meaningful categories to make it easier to identify tools and information relevant to specific organisms, research areas, applications, and AMR-related needs.

Search and Discovery

Find relevant AMR resources through the Hub's search and discovery features, reducing the need to navigate multiple websites and platforms when looking for specialized resources.

One Health Coverage

Discover resources spanning the human, animal, food, and environmental dimensions of antimicrobial resistance, supporting a broader One Health approach to AMR.

Centralized Access

AMRx Hub brings distributed AMR resources together in one platform, providing users with a single starting point for discovering tools and information that may otherwise be scattered across different websites and repositories.

Technical Features

How the platform is built and maintained.

Metadata-Driven Resource Management

Each resource is described using standardized metadata, supporting consistent classification and improving the discoverability and organization of AMR resources across the platform.

External Resource Integration

AMRx Hub can reference and connect users to tools, databases, publications, and other resources hosted on external platforms, allowing the Hub to function as a centralized discovery layer without requiring every resource to be hosted locally.

Search and Filtering Infrastructure

The platform incorporates search and filtering mechanisms designed to help users navigate and retrieve relevant resources from the growing AMR resource collection.

Modular and Extensible Design

The platform is built with a modular architecture, allowing for easy integration of new features and functionalities as the needs of the AMR community evolve.

Open Source and Community-Driven

AMRx Hub is developed as an open-source project, encouraging community contributions and fostering collaboration among researchers, developers, and practitioners in the AMR field.
