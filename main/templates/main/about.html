About AMRx Hub

Our Mission

Our mission is to make AMR tools easy to find, understand, and use — especially in resource-limited settings. We aim to support timely decisions, improve data analysis, and foster global collaboration in AMR surveillance and response.

Our Vision

To become the leading global platform for AMR tools—enabling timely access to practical digital solutions that empower health systems, support research, and accelerate coordinated action against antimicrobial resistance.

Who this is for?

Epidemiologists and public health officials

Use the tools to monitor AMR trends, compare resistance rates across regions, and support data-driven policy decisions.

Clinical microbiologists and laboratory professionals

Leverage data management and analysis tools to interpret laboratory results, detect resistance patterns, and streamline reporting processes.

Data scientists and informaticians

Use AMR datasets and APIs for advanced data modelling, visualization, and predictive analytics. They also help in customising or improving open-source tools for broader usability.

Academics and Students

Explore curated tools and datasets for learning, teaching, or researching antimicrobial resistance. The platform also links to training materials and peer-reviewed resources useful for theses or classroom instruction.

Bioinformaticians

Utilize genome analysis tools, sequence data interpretation platforms, and pipelines for identifying resistance genes. Their work bridges lab data with computational insights critical for tracking resistance evolution.

Healthcare providers and clinicians

Use clinical decision-support tools to choose appropriate antibiotics, interpret lab results, and stay informed about resistance trends relevant to patient care.

Key Features

Web-Based Interface

Accessible via any modern web browser, with no need for downloads or installations. Designed to be responsive and mobile-friendly.

Interactive Dashboards

Providing dynamic visualizations that allow users to explore data in real time. They support filtering, trend analysis, and customizable views, making complex information easier to understand and act upon.

Mobile Friendly

A mobile-friendly system ensuring that users can seamlessly interact with the platform on smartphones, tablets, and desktops without losing functionality or readability.

Technical Features

Centralized AMR Tool Repository

A unified, web-based platform that consolidates diverse AMR tools in one place, making them easy to find, compare, and access across different health systems and regions.

Scalable & Accessible Platform

Optimized for resource-limited settings, offering lightweight design, multilingual support, and the ability to handle datasets of varying size—ensuring inclusivity and scalability across regions.

Secure Platform

Implements strong data security, privacy protection, and compliance with international standards, safeguarding sensitive health information.
