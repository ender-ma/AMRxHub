 Privacy Policy AMRx Hub (the “Platform”) is committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with Nigeria’s Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the National Health Act 2014 (NHA). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how we safeguard and use it. AMRx Hub is based in Nigeria and serves as a directory of antimicrobial resistance (AMR) resources. We do not host or operate any AMR analysis tools ourselves, but we link to third-party tools, PDFs, and videos. By using our platform, you consent to the data practices described below. We have structured this policy to be clear and concise, following the NDPA’s requirement that privacy notices be clear, concise, transparent, intelligible, and easily accessible Information We Collect ● Account Information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, and a password. We verify your email to create a secure account. We use this information exclusively to manage your account, authenticate your identity, and communicate with you. You may update or delete this information through your account settings. ● Usage Data and Cookies: We automatically collect technical data (e.g., browser type, device type, pages visited, timestamp) to operate and improve the Platform. We also use cookies for essential site functions (e.g., maintaining your login session) and for performance analytics (such as Google Analytics) to understand usage patterns. Cookies may include session cookies (which expire when you close your browser) and persistent cookies (which remain until deleted). We explain cookie use in detail in our Cookie Policy. ● Other Information: We do not currently collect sensitive personal data (such as health or biometric data). Under the NHA, all health information 
 is conﬁdential, and we will handle any such data only with explicit consent and necessary safeguards. We practice data minimization: we only collect what is necessary. For example, we do not ask for health history or personal identiﬁers beyond what is needed for account registration and service delivery. All personal data collected is processed in compliance with the NDPA, which mandates that processing be for a speciﬁc, legitimate, and lawful purpose disclosed to the user How We Use Your Information We use your information for the following purposes: ● Account Management: To verify your email, secure your account, and provide you access to the platform's features. ● Communications: To send you important notices (e.g., password reset, updates to terms) and responses to any inquiries or support requests. ● Improving the Platform: We may use analytics to improve site functionality, troubleshoot issues, and optimize user experience. All analytics data are aggregated and anonymized where possible. ● Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws and regulations, such as responding to lawful data requests or enforcing our terms of service. We will not use your personal information for purposes incompatible with the NDPA without further consent. We process data lawfully according to the NDPA based on your consent, contract performance (our Terms of Service), legal obligations, or our legitimate interests (improving the Platform). In all cases, we ensure that processing is proportionate and respects your privacy. Legal Bases for Processing Under the NDPA, personal data processing is only lawful if one of the following bases applies: 
 1. Consent: You have given clear consent for us to process your data for speciﬁc purposes (e.g., setting up your account, sending newsletters). You may withdraw consent at any time, and we will stop processing data based on that consent. 2. Contract: Processing is necessary to perform our Terms of Service (e.g., to maintain your user account and provide the platform's directory services). 3. Legal Obligation: Processing is necessary to comply with legal requirements (e.g., retention of records for regulatory compliance). 4. Legitimate Interest: For platform administration and improvement, as long as it does not override your rights. We do not rely on sensitive bases (such as health) without explicit additional consent, and we do not sell or share your personal data without permission. We document the legal basis for any international transfers. Cookies and Tracking We use cookies and similar technologies as described in our Cookie Policy. Data Security We implement robust technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. For example, we use secure servers, encryption for data in transit, and access controls to limit who can access it. These measures are in line with NDPA requirements for security, integrity, and conﬁdentiality. We regularly review and update our security practices to guard against emerging threats. Despite these safeguards, no system is completely immune to breaches. In the unlikely event of a personal data breach, we will follow NDPA’s breach notiﬁcation rules. Speciﬁcally, we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) and affected users if a breach is likely to pose a high risk to your rights and freedoms. 
 Data Retention We retain your personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above or as required by law. We will securely erase or anonymize your personal information when you delete your account or it remains inactive for an extended period. Retention periods may vary: for example, for support purposes, we may keep contact logs for a reasonable period (e.g., 2–3 years). NDPA requires that data be kept only as long as needed for its original purpose. If we have no legitimate reason to keep your data, we will remove it. Your Rights The NDPA grants you several rights over your personal data. These include the right to: ● Access and Rectiﬁcation: You can request conﬁrmation of whether we hold your personal data and access to a copy. You can also ask us to correct any inaccurate or incomplete data. ● Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): You may request deletion of your data when it is no longer needed for the purposes for which we collected it or if you have withdrawn consent and we have no other legal basis to retain it. ● Restriction: You can ask us to restrict or suspend processing of your data, for example, if you contest its accuracy or we are determining if we have a lawful basis. ● Data Portability: You may request a portable copy of your data in a structured, commonly used format. ● Objection: You are entitled to object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes. 
 ● Withdraw Consent: When processing is based on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time, and we will stop processing data for that purpose. ● Complain to Authorities: If you believe your data protection rights have been violated, you may lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) or other relevant authority. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below. We will respond in accordance with NDPA timelines and without unreasonable delay. Sensitive and Health-Related Information Although our platform does not collect health records, we recognize the special sensitivity of health information. We do not expect to handle patient-speciﬁc health records; however, if you provide any health-related information (for example, in feedback), we will treat it as sensitive personal data. We will secure it with additional safeguards and only process it with explicit consent, as required by law.
