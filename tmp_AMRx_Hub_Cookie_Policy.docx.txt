 AMRx Hub Cookie Policy AMRx Hub uses "cookies." Your browser stores small data ﬁles, known as cookies, to enhance the functionality of our site and understand its usage. Cookies remember things like your login or preferences and help us improve the site. Some cookies are strictly necessary for security and core functions; others (like analytics cookies) track usage. This policy explains the different types of cookies we use, why we use them, how long they last, and how you can manage your cookie preferences. We comply with Nigeria’s Data Protection Act (NDPA) and global best practices (e.g., the EU’s GDPR/ePrivacy rules), which require clear notice and consent for non-essential cookies. What Are Cookies? Cookies are small text ﬁles that websites send to your browser to store information about your visit. Your browser keeps these cookies and sends them back to the website on subsequent requests. In practice, cookies let AMRx Hub “remember” you: for example, keeping you logged in as you browse, saving items you’ve added to a cart or your site preferences, and recording which pages you visit. Some cookies are strictly required for the site to function (such as session or authentication cookies used to verify your login); others (like analytics cookies) collect data about your visit to help improve the platform or show you relevant content. Types of Cookies We Use We use the following categories of cookies on AMRx Hub. Essential cookies (required for security and basic operation) are on by default. Analytics cookies are only enabled if you choose to consent. 
 ● Essential (Strictly Necessary) Cookies: These cookies are required for core site functions such as user login, session security, and site navigation. For example, we use session cookies to keep you logged in as you move between pages. Because these cookies are essential to protect your account and the integrity of the site, they are always active and do not require your opt-in consent. Without them, parts of AMRx Hub would not work (e.g., you might have to log in on every page or encounter security errors). ● Analytics Cookies: These cookies collect information about how visitors use AMRx Hub—for example, which pages are most visited, how long users stay on each page, and which features are most used. We use Google Analytics as our analytics provider. Google Analytics sets cookies (such as the _ga cookie) to distinguish users and measure sessions. By default, Google Analytics’ _ga cookie expires after about 2 years, and its _gid cookie expires after 24 hours. This tracking is strictly anonymous (it does not record your name or personal details). AMRx Hub guarantees the exclusive use of our analytics cookies for site navigation and performance, given the sensitive scientiﬁc and health-related workﬂows we handle. They do not track, record, or monitor any speciﬁc health queries, uploaded diagnostic data, or clinical research inputs you process on the platform. Analytics cookies are not strictly necessary, so we will only place them if you agree. You can choose to enable or disable analytics cookies at any time in our cookie settings. (Any other cookies – such as “functional” cookies that remember your language or theme preferences – would also fall under non-essential cookies and require consent. They improve your experience but are not required for login or security.) Required vs. Optional Cookies: Required (essential) cookies are always enabled and do not need a user’s consent. All other cookies (such as analytics) are optional and will only run if you actively opt in. This follows Nigeria’s NDPA guidelines and international 
 standards: only strictly necessary cookies are exempt from consent, and all other cookies require your clear acceptance. Why We Use Cookies ● Security & Access: Essential cookies keep you securely logged in and prevent unauthorized access. They protect against attacks (like CSRF protection cookies) and remember when your login is valid. ● Functionality: Some cookies remember your preferences (e.g., chosen display language or layout) so you don’t have to reset them every visit. (These are non-essential convenience cookies; they will only be activated if you enable them in your preference settings.) ● Performance & Analytics: Cookies let us gather anonymous statistics about usage (via Google Analytics). This tells us which pages are popular and where we can improve the site. By analyzing trends, we can ﬁx issues faster and make the user experience better over time. Cookie Duration (Retention) How long a cookie stays on your device depends on its type: ● Session Cookies: Many essential cookies (like login/session cookies) are session cookies. They are deleted when you close your browser or log out of AMRx Hub. These cookies have no set expiration date and exist only for your current visit. ● Persistent Cookies: Analytics cookies are persistent cookies. They stay on your device even after you close the browser, for a deﬁned period (up to their expiration date). For example, Google Analytics’ _ga cookie persists for 2 years by default (though browsers may cap this time, e.g., Chrome limits it to about 400 days). Its _gid cookie lasts 24 hours. 
 We do not keep any cookie data longer than necessary. In line with NDPA principles, we retain personal data (including cookie identiﬁers) only as long as needed to provide the service. After a cookie expires or is deleted, it no longer tracks you. Third-Party Cookies and Partners We use trusted third-party service providers to help us analyze site usage and improve AMRx Hub. These providers may set cookies on your device on our behalf. ● Google Analytics (First-Party Cookies via a Third-Party Provider): We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage data. While Google is a third-party service, it sets ﬁrst-party cookies (such as _ga and _gid) directly on the AMRx Hub domain. These cookies are not used to track your activity across other, unaffiliated websites. Google's processing of this data is governed by Google’s privacy policies. ● Third-Party Cookies: A true third-party cookie belongs to a domain entirely different from AMRx Hub. Currently, we do not deploy third-party cookies for cross-site tracking or advertising. If this changes, we will update this policy and request your explicit consent. Third-party cookies follow the rules of their own companies. We do not control them directly, but we will always disclose their presence. Managing Your Preferences When you ﬁrst visit AMRx Hub, a cookie banner or pop-up will appear, letting you accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or customize your settings. You can also manage or delete cookies directly through your browser settings. For instructions on how to do so, please visit the official support pages for your speciﬁc browser (e.g., Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, or Microsoft Edge). 
 Turning off cookies may limit some features of AMRx Hub. For full control, use our site’s cookie settings panel (if available) or clear cookies via your browser at any time. You can also withdraw consent for non-essential cookies at any time; doing so will not affect the operation of essential cookies but may change your experience.
